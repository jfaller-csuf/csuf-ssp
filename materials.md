# OER Materials

All Open Educational Resources (OER) developed for the Summer Scholars Program are available at: [https://github.com/jfaller-csuf/csuf-ssp-oer](https://github.com/jfaller-csuf/csuf-ssp-oer)

## Overview

This repository contains openly available instructional materials developed to support semiconductor and computer engineering education for both **high school (HS)** students and **early undergraduate (CSUF)** students.

The materials are designed for use in lectures, labs, guided activities, and student projects. They may also be adapted by other educators for outreach programs, short courses, or introductory engineering instruction.

## Repository Contents

The repository includes materials such as:

- Lecture materials
- Laboratory activities
- Student worksheets
- Example code
- Instructor support materials
- Supplemental resources

## Topics

The full list of SSP topics is available here: [https://jfaller-csuf.github.io/csuf-ssp/topics.html](https://jfaller-csuf.github.io/csuf-ssp/topics.html)

## Purpose

The goal of these OER materials is to make semiconductor and computer engineering education more accessible by providing reusable, adaptable, and freely available learning resources.

# **CSUF Semiconductor Summer Program (SSP)**

A 9-week summer experience in semiconductors, chip design, and career readiness for early CSUF ECE Undergraduate students and High School (HS) students

## **Program Theme**

**“How a Program Becomes Hardware”**

CSUF Students progress from:

Understanding chips and logic → to designing processor components → to implementing and integrating simple system(s) in Verilog → to preparing a design for fabrication

HS Students progress from:

Understanding chips and logic → to seeing how instructions map to hardware operations → to tracing how hardware executes programs step-by-step → to explaining complete system behavior

## Key details
- **When:** Summer 2026
- **Duration:** 9 weeks 
- **Time Commitment:** 
    - ~20 hours/week (CSUF)
    - ~10 hours/week (HS)
- **Locations:** 
    - CSUF Campus (CSUF)
    - Virtual (HS)
- **Cohort:** 
    - 20 HS Students
    - 20 CSUF Students

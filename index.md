# **CSUF Semiconductor Summer Program (SSP)**

A 9-week summer experience in semiconductors, chip design, and career readiness for early CSUF ECE Undergraduate students and High School (HS) students

## Key details
- **When:** Summer 2026
- **Duration:** 9 weeks 
- **Time Commitment:** ~5-10 hours/week
- **Cohort:** 
    - 20 HS Students
    - 20 CSUF Students
